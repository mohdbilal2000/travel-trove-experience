Warm greetings from Guide India Tours. We are delighted to present a private five-day Golden Triangle journey for four guests — Delhi, Agra and Jaipur, with a private air-conditioned vehicle and professional chauffeur, a government-approved tourist guide, all monument entrance tickets and the elephant ride at Amber Fort included.

02 — Itinerary

Your Journey

1
Day One · Thu 25 Feb · Arrival Delhi
Arrival and light Delhi sightseeing
You are met by your Guide India Tours representative at Delhi Airport and transferred privately to your hotel — approximately 15–30 km, 45–90 minutes depending on hotel location and traffic. Depending on your arrival time, light sightseeing across the capital: India Gate and the Parliament area, Humayun’s Tomb and Qutub Minar.

Overnight in Delhi.
02 — Itinerary continued

Your Journey

2
Day Two · Fri 26 Feb · Delhi → Agra
Old Delhi, then down to Agra
After breakfast: Jama Masjid, the Red Fort, the lanes of Old Delhi and Raj Ghat. Then the drive to Agra by the Yamuna Expressway — approximately 230 km, 3.5–4 hours.

On arrival, Agra Fort, Itmad-ud-Daulah (the Baby Taj) and Mehtab Bagh for the Taj Mahal viewpoint, subject to timing. Local sightseeing is roughly 20–30 km in total, with 15–30 minutes between sites depending on traffic.

Overnight in Agra.
3
Day Three · Sat 27 Feb · Agra → Jaipur
The Taj Mahal, Fatehpur Sikri and on to Jaipur
Early morning at the Taj Mahal with your government-approved professional tourist guide. After breakfast and check-out, the drive towards Jaipur.

Fatehpur Sikri en route — approximately 40 km, one hour from Agra — for the Buland Darwaza, the Jama Masjid and the royal palace complex. Then on to Jaipur, approximately 205 km and 4–4.5 hours.

Agra to Jaipur by way of Fatehpur Sikri is roughly 245 km in total, plus sightseeing.

Overnight in Jaipur.
4
Day Four · Sun 28 Feb · Jaipur
A full day in the royal city
Amber Fort — approximately 11 km from the city, 30–45 minutes — with your elephant ride included. Then Jal Mahal (8 km, 20–30 minutes), the City Palace (6 km, 20–30 minutes), Jantar Mantar next door and Hawa Mahal a short transfer away. Later Nahargarh Fort, 12–15 km and 30–45 minutes depending on traffic and road conditions.

An authentic experience of local art, handicrafts and live demonstrations along the way. Shopping is entirely optional — there is no forced shopping on any of our tours.

Overnight in Jaipur.
5
Day Five · Mon 1 Mar · Departure
A relaxed morning, then your onward transfer
After breakfast, a relaxed morning before your transfer. Two drop options:

Option 1 — Jaipur. Your hotel to Jaipur Airport, approximately 10–15 km, 30–60 minutes depending on hotel location and traffic.

Option 2 — Delhi. Jaipur to Delhi Airport, approximately 260–280 km, 5–6 hours depending on traffic and road conditions.

Your private chauffeur stays with you to the end of the journey. End of services.
