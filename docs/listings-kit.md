## Wikipedia

Do not add the business or its link to Wikipedia. Wikipedia's rules treat editing about your own
business as a conflict of interest, promotional additions are reverted, and external links there
carry `nofollow`, so they pass no ranking value anyway. A company article needs independent
coverage in reliable sources first. The workable route to that coverage is press and travel-writer
mentions, which then make a neutral article possible for someone with no connection to the company.
