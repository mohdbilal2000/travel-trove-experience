# WhatsApp Auto-Reply Messages — Guide India Tours

Set these up in **WhatsApp Business → Settings → Business tools**:

- **Greeting message** — sent automatically the first time someone messages you
  (or after 14 days of no activity). Use Template 1.
- **Away message** — sent when a message arrives outside working hours or while
  you're busy. Use Template 2.

⚠️ **Greeting and away messages are limited to 200 characters** — both templates
below fit within that limit (counts noted). Quick Replies (Template 3) have no
such limit.

---

## Template 1 · Greeting message (191 characters)

```
🙏 Namaste! Thanks for contacting Guide India Tours. A travel expert will reply shortly. For a faster quote, fill our 1-min form — it lands right back in this chat: guideindiatours.com/contact
```

---

## Template 2 · Away message (187 characters)

```
🙏 Thanks for contacting Guide India Tours! We reply within 2 hours, 24/7. Meanwhile, fill our 1-min form for a faster quote — it lands right back in this chat: guideindiatours.com/contact
```

---

## Template 3 · Quick reply for form leads (website inquiries)

Visitors who submit the website contact form or trip planner land in your chat
with their details already filled in. Save this as a **Quick Reply**
(shortcut suggestion: `/thanks`) and send it as your first manual response:

```
🙏 *Thank you for your inquiry, [NAME]!*

We've received your details and our travel expert is already working on the best options for you.

You'll receive a *personalised itinerary & quote* here on WhatsApp shortly.

In the meantime, is there anything special you'd love to include? (sunrise at the Taj, food walks, photography stops, shopping…)

_Guide India Tours · ⭐ 4.9/5 · 403+ Google Reviews · Since 2004_
```
